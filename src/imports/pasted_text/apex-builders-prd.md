# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

# Project Name

Apex Modern Builders

## Project Overview

Apex Modern Builders is a premium luxury home décor and interior styling website designed to showcase elegant residential transformations and allow potential clients to book design and construction services online.

The website should communicate sophistication, craftsmanship, and trust while maintaining a modern minimalist aesthetic.

The entire website should feel like a luxury architecture and interior design brand. Large imagery, generous white space, elegant typography, smooth animations, and a refined light yellow and white smoke color palette should communicate exclusivity and premium craftsmanship.
